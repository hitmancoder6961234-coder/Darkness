About The Project

This website is a professional market research platform designed to collect laptop-related survey data. Visitors answer questions based on whether they currently own a laptop or plan to purchase one. Responses are securely stored in Google Sheets and later transformed into percentage-based charts for analysis.

Features

Dynamic Survey

Questions automatically change depending on whether the participant owns a laptop.

Google Sheets Database

Every survey response is automatically stored inside Google Sheets.

Interactive Dashboard

Analyze responses through charts, graphs and percentage reports.

Responsive Design

Works smoothly on desktops, tablets and smartphones.
